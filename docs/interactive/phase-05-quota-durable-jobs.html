Phase 5

Quota and durable jobs

Job creation is now a fast, owner-scoped enqueue operation backed by PostgreSQL. Assessment-attempt identity is materialized during catalog refresh, active job tuples are unique, quota is reserved atomically, and workers can claim work with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED.

Idempotency: active jobs are unique for (user_id, lms_quiz_id, lms_results_id) in queued/running/paused states. Existing active jobs are returned as receipts without charging quota again.

Quota: one assessment unit is reserved per newly accepted job in the same transaction. The conditional bucket update prevents concurrent oversubscription.

Timed safety: only explicit timedQuizStatus = no can enqueue; yes and unknown are POLICY_BLOCKED.

Queue: claim is a short transaction: lock one eligible row, mark it running with lease/heartbeat metadata, commit, then process outside the transaction.
