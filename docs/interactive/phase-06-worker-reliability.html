Phase 6

Worker reliability, leases, and recovery

The Worker is now a real queue consumer. It serializes each LMS account, respects a shared global rate-limit state, materializes question manifests once, checkpoints each durable question, pauses typed blockers, resumes safely, and recovers expired leases after crashes.

No long transactions: claiming and checkpoints are separate short transactions; LMS/AI calls occur outside them.

Account serialization: a compare-and-set PostgreSQL lease prevents concurrent workers racing one rotating LMS session.

Manifest rule: question identity is validated before insertion; positions and external IDs/fingerprints are unique. Replay skips durable questions.

Recovery: expired running leases are returned to queued with a stable WORKER_LEASE_EXPIRED marker.
