Ownership: subjects are read through the user's single LMS connection; assessment endpoints first prove subject ownership.

Identity: subject and assessment external IDs remain strings. Database unique constraints make repeated refresh an update, not duplication.

Status separation: LMS source text is preserved while Hallili separately derives NOT_STARTED, IN_PROGRESS, DRAFT_READY, or SUBMITTED.

Timed safety: unknown and yes are visibly blocked for solving by default.
